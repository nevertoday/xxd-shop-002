# XXD Shop 002 · Original brief (English reading translation)

> Reading translation only. The canonical runtime prompt is [zh-CN.md](zh-CN.md).

A signature restaurant-product e-commerce main image, premium food-service commercial visual, built on the logic of "remember the product name first, then feel appetite, then quickly form a purchase judgement".

If the user provides a dish photo, product photo, packaging shot or brand reference, it is the highest-priority authority: faithfully keep the product's own shape, plating, structure, tableware, colour, brand marks and key identifying features, without restyling or substituting it, and only on that basis rebuild a more premium, more unified, more saleable key visual. If no image is provided, generate it naturally from the dish name, category, regional flavour, core ingredients, consumption scene and brand character.

The image must feature one signature product, with the food as the absolute centre. Use a strongly appetite-driven close or medium-close view that clearly shows steam, glossiness, sauce, texture, layers and the relationship between main and secondary ingredients, so the viewer knows at first glance that this is the house's featured product. Tableware, trays, tabletops, hands or partial scenes may appear in moderation to add realism, support and a sense of lived-in warmth, but must not distract from the subject.

The product name must be the first point of visual recognition. The large title is not ordinary copy but product-name lettering with signboard, brand and graphic character. The letterforms need clear outlines, distinct personality, strong memorability and good legibility, readable at a glance even as a thumbnail. Keep clear contrast between title and background, so the dish name grabs attention first and then leads the eye to the food.

Recommendation stickers, stamps, tags or emblem systems suited to the product may be added naturally to reinforce sales judgements such as "signature, featured, best-seller, flavour, handmade, word of mouth, regional speciality, new, limited, classic, must-order, popular". Sticker content is not fixed; it should be generated intelligently from the product's attributes, brand voice and consumption scene, in moderate number with clear priority, like part of a signage system rather than a screenful of promotions. They may feel like seals, handwritten tags, round badges, banners, small emblems, corner marks, title slips, recommendation stamps or flavour tags, but must match the overall style — eye-catching without breaking the premium feel.

Supporting text recedes naturally and may include short selling lines, flavour notes, regional tags, brand phrases, an English subtitle or brief explanations, forming a clear editorial hierarchy: "big type with impact, stickers with judgement, small text with order".

The background stays restrained, building the environment with colour, space and atmosphere that bring out the product's flavour. It may lean rich, fiery and smoky, or fresh and refined, but always serves the product name and the food. Steam, halos, tabletop materials, traditional patterns, regional symbols or handmade traces may appear in moderation, all giving way to the subject.

Light serves "delicious" and "signature". Use directional soft light to shape the food's volume, freshness, heat, sheen and layering; highlights stir appetite, shadows keep detail, and not every element is lit equally. Make the food tempting, the text clear, and the stickers noticeable without jumping out.

The overall style should have: signboard feel, hit-product feel, brand feel, memorability and selling power. The viewer should know at a glance what is sold, feel at a glance that it is delicious, judge at a glance that it is the featured item worth ordering, and remember its name.

Avoid: a product name that is too small, characterless type, food that is not tempting enough, too many or messy stickers, an overly promotional feel, a busy background, fragmented information, piled-up props, an overly staged look, a weakened brand, and every element competing equally for attention.
